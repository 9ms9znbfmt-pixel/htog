HtoG comes from hoontography — a photo account I once used. This site is a quiet archive of images that stayed with me.

My work begins with immersion. I am drawn to moments when strong sunlight cuts through shadow, when light and darkness meet and a scene forms quietly in between.

Such moments are rarely announced. So I tend to wait rather than rush. I set a frame and wait for the final element to enter: a slight shift of light, a passing figure, or the moment when the scene settles on its own.

This is why I return to the same places. Sunlight may seem familiar, but it never arrives in the same way.

These photographs are shaped less by intention and more by instinct.

This site is not a conclusion, but a place where what remains after looking is allowed to stay.
